Our Story

Built on Trust, Driven by Excellence

Founded in 2010, Shuman Bookkeeping & Tax Preparation started with a simple mission: to provide small businesses with the same level of financial expertise that large corporations enjoy.

What began as a one-person operation has grown into a team of dedicated professionals, but our core values remain the same. We believe in building lasting relationships with our clients, taking the time to understand their unique needs, and delivering personalized service that makes a real difference.

Today, we're proud to serve over 500 businesses across the tri-state area, helping them navigate the complexities of bookkeeping, taxation, and financial planning.
