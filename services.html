Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our services.

How do I get started?

Simply fill out the contact form or call us. We'll schedule a free 15-minute consultation to discuss your needs and provide a custom quote.

Do you work with my industry?

Yes! We work with retail, restaurants, construction, e-commerce, professional services, and more. Every business is unique—we tailor our services to your specific needs.

What software do you use?

We use QuickBooks Online (QBO) as our primary platform—it's cloud-based, secure, and gives you 24/7 access to your financial data.

Is there a long-term contract?

No long-term contracts required. Our bookkeeping services are month-to-month—we earn your business every month.

Can you help with IRS problems?

Absolutely. We represent clients before the IRS for audits, collections, and tax notices. We'll handle the communication and work to resolve the issue.

How quickly can you start?

Typically within 5-7 business days from signing. We handle all the onboarding details to ensure a smooth transition.
